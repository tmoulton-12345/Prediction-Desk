## How a price was chosen

Polymarket US and Novig both list this Sunday MLB slate. An ask is the price to buy that side. On Polymarket US the long side is the best ask, and the short side is one minus the best bid. On Novig the ask is one minus the other outcome's best bid. American odds are rounded from that ask.

Polymarket US fee coefficient on these full-game markets is 0.0695, applied as coefficient times price times (1 minus price) per contract. Novig's fee on these markets is charged `WHEN_LIVE` only, so a pregame take is $0. When both venues price the same side, the card uses the ask that is better after that Polymarket fee. A Covers or CBS number is used only when it pays more than both venue asks.

Covers was last updated Sep 26, 2026, 10:06 p.m. ET. The sportsbook number cited from Covers is the first book column, converted from the fraction on the page. CBS is the second board. Where CBS prints a different total on each row, the total on the card is the Covers number.

Paper stake is $20. Profit on a plus-money price is $20 times the price over 100. Profit on a minus price is $20 times 100 over the absolute price. A loss is $20. If a run line or total had no juice, the assumption would be -110, which pays $18.18 on a $20 win. Every priced ticket below has a real juice, so -110 is not used.

Hit, Actual, and P/L stay blank for scoring after the games.
